Why the U.S.–Mexico corridor

$872.8B

U.S.–Mexico goods trade in 2025, up from roughly $839.9B in 2024.

#1

Mexico is the largest U.S. goods trading partner — about 16.4% of total U.S. goods trade.

50,000+

U.S. SMEs exported more than $107B in goods to Mexico in 2023.

USMCA

The 2026 joint review remains unresolved. The agreement stays in force while issues are worked through.

Sources: USTR Mexico trade profile (2025 goods trade); U.S. Census Bureau Top Trading Partners (2026 year-to-date); USTR/Commerce/SBA USMCA SME Dialogue (Nov. 2025); USTR statement on the July 1, 2026 USMCA joint review.

The problem

SMEs don’t lack software. They lack an integrated decision and trust layer.

Large enterprises absorb cross-border complexity with compliance teams, analysts and established logistics networks. Smaller companies carry the same requirements without the same infrastructure.

Market intelligence

Unclear foreign demand, buyer quality, price expectations and channel strategy.

Compliance

HS classification, origin rules, documentation and changing policy create real risk.

Counterparty trust

Limited confidence in buyers, distributors, suppliers and service providers.

Execution

Email, PDFs, spreadsheets and phone calls leave weak visibility into status.

Partner discovery

Finding qualified brokers, forwarders and distributors stays relationship-heavy.

How we think about AI

Automate the routine. Escalate the consequential.

Enduro IQ is designed to automate routine analysis while clearly identifying uncertainty. High-risk classification, customs, sanctions, legal and financial questions are escalated to qualified professionals or licensed partners. We treat that as product quality and liability discipline, not a limitation.

  • Retrieval from attributable sources before generation
  • Structured outputs for classification and extraction
  • Confidence thresholds and escalation for high-risk cases
  • Audit trail from source to model output to human decision

Enduro Solutions is a software and intelligence company. We are not a customs broker, a freight forwarder or a lender. Those functions are handled through licensed partners.
