Why Enduro

Cross-border trade runs on fragmented information.

Every company in this corridor has increasingly capable technology. The hardest problems happen between organizations, not inside them — critical information sits across government websites, emails, TMS platforms, customs systems, carrier portals, spreadsheets and personal relationships. Someone on your team spends their morning assembling a picture that falls apart by the afternoon.

Where the answer currently lives

  • Government and port-of-entry websites, each with its own format and refresh rate
  • Email threads, WhatsApp groups and phone calls asking what is happening right now
  • A TMS, customs software and customer portals that each see one slice
  • Spreadsheets and institutional memory held by two or three people

Enduro brings the signals together

We are not replacing the systems your business already runs. Enduro sits above them and answers one question:

“What is happening, what does it mean for my operation, and what should I pay attention to?”

Your TMS, ERP and customs software keep doing their jobs. Enduro makes them — and the corridor around them — more understandable.

Why the U.S.–Mexico corridor

$872.8B

U.S.–Mexico goods trade in 2025, up from roughly $839.9B in 2024.

#1

Mexico is the largest U.S. goods trading partner — about 16.4% of total U.S. goods trade.

$354B

Trade crossing Laredo — the #1 inland port in the U.S. and #3 nationally.

USMCA

The 2026 joint review remains unresolved. The agreement stays in force while issues are worked through.

Sources: USTR Mexico trade profile (2025 goods trade); U.S. Census Bureau Top Trading Partners (2026 year-to-date); Laredo Economic Development Corporation; USTR statement on the July 1, 2026 USMCA joint review.

How it works

Three steps, and none of them are your job.

01

We collect the signals.

Enduro continuously gathers information from border infrastructure, government sources, industry sources, partners and operators.

02

We make the signals useful.

Enduro organizes that fragmented information into one operating picture of the corridor, with the context that tells you whether a number is normal or not.

03

You know when something matters.

Monitor the crossings, markets and partners that matter to your operation, and get alerted when conditions change.
