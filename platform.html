Enduro IQ — in development

A guided market-entry and transaction-readiness workspace.

Version one is deliberately narrow: one end-to-end workflow for U.S. SMEs trading with Mexico, built and validated with design partners before it is broadened.

The workflow

Six steps, each with a concrete output.

Every stage produces something the company can act on or hand to a partner — not a dashboard to interpret.

  1. 01

    Profile

    Company, product, target state or region, and current trade experience.

    OutputStructured exporter profile

  2. 02

    Assess

    Market attractiveness, product classification cues, origin and compliance checklist, risk factors.

    OutputExport readiness and market-entry brief

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Extract and validate uploaded commercial documents; identify missing data before it becomes a delay.

    OutputTransaction and document readiness status

  4. 04

    Connect

    Surface the partner types the transaction actually requires, and candidate providers for each.

    OutputBroker, forwarder and distributor shortlist

  5. 05

    Execute

    Track milestones, decisions, exceptions and partner handoffs in one place instead of a mail thread.

    OutputShared transaction workspace

  6. 06

    Learn

    Capture outcomes and feedback so the next transaction starts further along than the last.

    OutputCorridor intelligence

Scope discipline

What Enduro IQ is not.

These are deliberate exclusions, not gaps in a roadmap. Narrow scope is how a small team ships something that actually works.

  • Not an asset-owning freight or logistics company.

  • Not a lender. We do not take balance-sheet credit risk.

  • Not a customs broker. Brokerage, customs, payments and financing are handled through licensed partners.

  • Not making autonomous customs decisions without human review.

  • Not built on blockchain. We have found no requirement for it.

  • Not attempting global country coverage before one corridor has repeatable economics.

How the AI works

General-purpose AI is not the moat. Attributable data and supervised workflows are.

AI is the reasoning and automation layer on top of differentiated data, workflows and feedback loops — not a substitute for any of them.

Retrieval before generation

Answers are grounded in attributable sources, so a claim can be traced back to where it came from.

Structured outputs

Classification, document extraction and decision checkpoints return structured data, not prose to re-key.

Confidence thresholds

High-risk classification, customs, sanctions, legal and financial questions escalate to qualified professionals.

Audit trail

Source, model output, human decision and subsequent outcome are all recorded.

Customer data stays the customer’s

We do not train generalized models on customer-confidential data without explicit contractual permission.

Evaluated by task

Model performance is measured per task and per corridor, not by generic benchmark scores.

On verification and trust signals

Enduro IQ will provide explainable verification indicators for counterparties. We are not marketing a composite trust score. A single score is only responsible once data coverage, bias controls and legal review support it, and we are not there.
