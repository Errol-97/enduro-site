How Enduro works

An intelligence layer above the systems you already run.

You already have a TMS, customs software, spreadsheets, email, customer portals, government websites, carrier systems and people calling each other asking what is happening. Enduro’s job is to bring the relevant signals together and tell you what they mean.

The model

Signal, context, relevance.

A wait time on its own is a number. A wait time you can compare against normal, attached to the crossing you actually use, delivered before you dispatch, is a decision.

  1. 01

    We collect the signals.

    Enduro continuously gathers information from border infrastructure, government sources, industry sources, partners and operators — including things only a person standing at the crossing would know.

    OutputA corridor dataset that gets denser every week

  2. 02

    We make the signals useful.

    Enduro organizes fragmented information into one operating picture, with the context that distinguishes a normal Tuesday from a deteriorating one.

    OutputOne operating picture instead of eleven tabs

  3. 03

    You know when something matters.

    You tell Enduro which crossings, lanes, commodities and partners matter. Enduro watches them and tells you when something material changes.

    OutputAlerts and a recurring intelligence brief

The difference this makes

A dashboard is something a person has to remember to check. A watchlist is a system watching the corridor on your behalf. We are building the second thing.

What you get

Five parts, one subscription.

01

Corridor Monitor

A live operating view of the border: crossing wait times, port-of-entry conditions, extended-hours availability, closures, infrastructure events, weather risk, policy developments, unusual congestion and the historical baseline to compare any of it against.

02

Intelligence Feed

The same information, interpreted. Not “World Trade Bridge wait: 87 minutes,” but congestion elevated 42% above the normal Tuesday range, northbound commercial traffic deteriorating, and a suggested alternate crossing window.

03

Watchlists & Alerts

Follow specific crossings, cities, corridors, commodities, carriers, customers, facilities and suppliers. Enduro alerts you when a watched condition changes materially — not every time a number moves.

04

Trade Network Intelligence

A searchable directory and intelligence graph of the trade ecosystem: customs brokers, carriers, drayage providers, forwarders, warehouses, industrial parks, ports of entry, manufacturers, suppliers, agencies and logistics providers.

05

Enduro Intelligence Brief

A recurring executive summary of your corridor — what moved, what is now elevated, which policy announcements affect commercial traffic, and one operational recommendation for the week. Briefs are analyst-reviewed before they go out, which is why they read like something a person wrote rather than a digest nobody opens.

Decisions

Trade data tells you what traded. That is not the same thing.

Customs and shipment-record providers describe transactions after the fact. They do not tell you why a crossing deteriorated this morning, which warehouse is constrained, which drayage operator is reliable, where the handoff broke, or what you should do in the next hour. We are neutral infrastructure for that question — keep your existing logistics partners and systems; we help you understand and coordinate the network around them.

Integration is a mechanism, not the product

Connecting to a TMS, ERP or CRM can become strategically important, and we expect it to. But we are not selling integration work. Every connector exists to enrich the intelligence layer, and it gets built when a customer’s intelligence is measurably better for having it.

Scope discipline

What Enduro is not.

These are deliberate exclusions, not gaps in a roadmap. Narrow scope is how a small team ships something that actually works.

  • Not a TMS, ERP or brokerage platform. We are not asking you to replace the systems you run.

  • Not a freight marketplace. We do not book loads, dispatch carriers or take a cut of the move.

  • Not an asset-owning freight or logistics company.

  • Not a customs broker or a lender. Brokerage, customs, payments and financing stay with licensed partners.

  • Not full shipment visibility, predictive ETAs or guaranteed wait-time reductions. Those sit outside the current pilot.

  • Not attempting global coverage before one corridor has repeatable economics.

How the AI works

General-purpose AI is not the moat. Attributable data and supervised workflows are.

AI is the reasoning and automation layer on top of differentiated data, workflows and feedback loops — not a substitute for any of them. An alert you cannot trace back to a source is not intelligence.

Retrieval before generation

Answers are grounded in attributable sources, so a claim can be traced back to where it came from.

Structured outputs

Conditions, events and network records are structured data, not prose to re-key.

Confidence thresholds

High-risk classification, customs, sanctions, legal and financial questions escalate to qualified professionals.

Audit trail

Source, model output, human decision and subsequent outcome are all recorded.

Customer data stays the customer’s

We do not train generalized models on customer-confidential data without explicit contractual permission.

Evaluated by task

Model performance is measured per task and per corridor, not by generic benchmark scores.

On verification and counterparty scoring

Knowing who you are handing freight to is part of knowing what is happening in the corridor. As the network graph fills in, Enduro Network will carry verification and performance scoring for carriers, brokers, warehouses and other operators — built on observed corridor behaviour rather than self-reported profiles, and explainable down to the signals behind a score. That capability follows the dataset; it is not in the current pilot.
