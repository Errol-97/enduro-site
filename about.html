About

Make cross-border commerce accessible, explainable and executable.

That is the mission, and the order matters. Accessible without explainable is a black box. Explainable without executable is a report nobody acts on.

Long-term vision

The intelligence layer for global trade, corridor by corridor.

We are investigating how technology and AI can reduce the information and coordination burden SMEs face entering and operating in the U.S.–Mexico corridor — and then whether what we learn there transfers.

Rather than attempting to model every jurisdiction from day one, we intend to accumulate deep operational and transaction intelligence in one corridor, codify what works, and reuse the architecture, data contracts and partner playbook in adjacent ones.

We are deliberately not a consulting shop. Human trade expertise is used to validate, supervise and improve the product — not to substitute for productizing it.

Strategic boundaries

What we have decided not to be.

Not asset-owning

We are not a logistics company and do not intend to own freight assets.

Not AI-as-moat

AI is the reasoning layer on top of differentiated data, workflows and feedback loops.

Not a consultancy

Expertise validates and improves the product rather than replacing productization.

Partner-enabled

Brokerage, customs, payments and financing run through licensed partners.

Founder

Errol Rochester

Founder, Enduro Solutions

  • Enterprise solution architect since 2019
  • IMBA candidate — Operations & Supply Chain
  • B.S. Computer Science, summa cum laude
  • Previously co-founder and CTO of a mobile marketplace

Enduro IQ sits at the intersection of three things Errol has spent his career on separately: making fragmented enterprise systems talk to each other, the operations and supply-chain discipline to know which handoffs actually matter, and the experience of taking a product from nothing to launch.

He has worked as a solution architect and engineer since 2019 — at Capgemini, Capital One, ManTech on a Department of Defense contract, and currently at IDEMIA and Trucordia. The recurring assignment has been the one Enduro IQ describes: systems that each hold a different piece of the truth, data that has to move between them without being re-keyed or lost, and the governance to know where a given record came from. He is a Salesforce Certified Application Architect and Data Architect among eight certifications, and has led integrations across CRM, ERP and inventory-management systems through middleware — structurally the same problem a cross-border transaction presents.

He is completing an International MBA at the Darla Moore School of Business, University of South Carolina, concentrating in Operations & Supply Chain with a Business Analytics certificate. Before that, a B.S. in Computer Science from South Carolina State University, summa cum laude, and two first-place hackathon finishes. He was most recently co-founder and CTO of Custie, a two-sided mobile marketplace he took from architecture through launch on iOS and Android, including payments, go-to-market and investor materials.

What he is not is a customs broker or a twenty-year trade veteran. That gap is the reason for the discovery work rather than an argument against it: the technical and operational half of this problem he knows well, and the corridor half he intends to learn from the people who run it.

Enduro Solutions LLC was organized in 2022. The trade-intelligence work that became Enduro IQ grew out of earlier efforts under the Trilink Global Trade name; that work has been consolidated under Enduro Solutions, with a narrower product thesis and a single corridor to prove it in.

Current stage

Being clear about what exists and what does not.

Enduro IQ is in development. We are running customer discovery and recruiting design partners. We are not selling software yet, and this site is not a product announcement.

We do not hold institutional partnership agreements. Where we describe relationships with government, university, port or industry organizations, we mean conversations, introductions or prospective channels — and we will say so plainly until a written agreement exists.

Trade figures on this site are attributed to their sources where they appear. Where we do not have defensible data — market sizing, for instance — we do not publish a number.
