For trade partners

We are not trying to replace you. We are trying to make you easier to find and better informed.

Enduro Solutions is a software and intelligence company. We do not book loads, dispatch carriers, clear customs or take a cut of the move. Brokerage, customs, freight, payments and financing are executed by licensed professionals — and we would rather work with the ones already doing it well.

The exchange

What each side gets.

You spend part of every day answering the same question your customers could answer themselves if the information were in one place: what is the border doing right now? That is the part software can genuinely fix — and Trade Network Intelligence is a directory that lists providers like you, not a competitor to them.

What you get

  • Corridor intelligence and disruption alerts for the crossings you run
  • Visibility in a directory your customers actually search
  • Fewer “what is happening at the bridge?” phone calls to field
  • Customers who arrive already knowing the conditions

What we get

  • Ground truth about how the corridor actually runs
  • Signals no public feed publishes — what you can see from the yard
  • Execution capacity we have no intention of building ourselves
  • A reality check on assumptions made from the outside

Who we’re talking to

Four kinds of relationship.

Execution partners

Customs brokers, freight forwarders, carriers and 3PLs.

You know things about this corridor that no public data source publishes — which bridge backs up first, what a closure actually does to a schedule, which signals are noise. That is the difference between a wait time and intelligence.

Institutional and channel

Chambers, economic-development organizations, incubators and universities.

You already sit where corridor operators congregate, and you already field the question “who can help me with this crossing?” We would like to understand what those companies come to you unable to see for themselves.

Data and domain

Trade attorneys, compliance specialists, researchers and industry groups.

Our approach depends on attributable sources and expert review of high-risk cases. We are interested in data relationships and in people willing to tell us when a model output is wrong.

Strategic and regulatory

Trade associations, port authorities, banks and public-sector trade professionals.

Infrastructure decisions, operating hours, policy changes and financing structures all move the corridor. We would rather understand that structure early than discover it late.

Where we actually are

How we describe relationships.

Enduro IQ is in pilot with corridor operators, and partners shape what it monitors. Where we describe relationships with government, university, port or industry organizations, we mean conversations, introductions or prospective channels until a written agreement exists. We would rather be precise than impressive.

What we are looking for is organizations willing to talk candidly about how this corridor actually works, and to keep working with us past the first conversation.

Referral economics, directory listings, integrations and formal agreements get built as the relationship earns them.
