Who we are

Enduro Solutions is building Enduro IQ.

Enduro IQ is a cross-border trade intelligence platform for small and midsize companies operating between the U.S. and Mexico — bringing market intelligence, compliance, partner discovery and transaction execution into one guided workflow instead of a dozen disconnected ones.

It is in active development. We are not selling software at this symposium. We are here to learn from the people who move goods across this border every day, and to find out where our assumptions are wrong.

What we’re asking people this week

Four questions. Honest answers welcome.

  1. 01

    What are the three ugliest recurring problems in U.S.–Mexico trade for smaller companies?

  2. 02

    Where does information get duplicated, lost, manually re-entered, delayed or misunderstood between exporter, forwarder, broker, carrier and importer?

  3. 03

    What do your SME clients repeatedly misunderstand — and what do you have to ask them for over and over again?

  4. 04

    If you were building technology for SMEs entering this corridor, what would you insist we understand before writing a line of code?
