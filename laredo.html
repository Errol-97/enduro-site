Who we are

Enduro monitors the trade corridor for you.

Enduro Solutions is building Enduro IQ, a cross-border trade intelligence platform. We bring together border conditions, disruptions, operational signals and trade-network information so the people running cross-border freight can see what is happening in one place instead of assembling it from a dozen sources every morning.

We are early and we will be direct about it. The pilot described below is what exists and what we are committing to build with our first customers — not a finished product with a login. If that is the wrong fit for you, we would still like the conversation.

Enduro Laredo Intelligence Pilot

90 days. One corridor. Built with you, not at you.

For cross-border operators moving freight through Laredo — carriers, brokers, 3PLs, warehouses and manufacturers.

What the pilot includes

  • Laredo crossing-condition dashboard
  • World Trade Bridge monitoring
  • Colombia Solidarity Bridge monitoring
  • Corridor disruption alerts
  • A personalized operational watchlist
  • Weekly intelligence brief
  • Trade-network directory
  • Access to Enduro’s developing corridor dataset
  • Direct founder support
  • The ability to influence the product roadmap

The outcome we are accountable for

Reduce the time your team spends searching for border information, and increase visibility into the conditions affecting your cross-border operation.

What we are not promising yet

We would rather lose a deal than oversell one. These are real product ambitions, and none of them are part of this pilot.

  • Perfect shipment visibility
  • Guaranteed wait-time reductions
  • Predictive ETAs
  • Full TMS integration
  • End-to-end supply-chain orchestration

Pricing

We are setting pilot pricing with the first customers rather than publishing a number we have not tested. Ask and you will get a straight answer in the conversation, not a quote three weeks later.
